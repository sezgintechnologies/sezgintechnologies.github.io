Privacy Policy

Virtual Paw · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Virtual Paw (“the game”) is made by Sezgin Technologies (“we”). The game is designed for players of all ages, including children. We take privacy seriously and collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location, and there are no accounts or chat.
  • Your pet and progress are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob. They are set up for children: no personalised advertising, no advertising ID and family-friendly content only.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your pet, home, coins, settings and progress in a file on your device so that you can continue later. This file never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game, or choosing Settings → Start over, deletes it.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, “your pet is hungry”) directly on your device. No push service is used and no information is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, you can choose to watch a short video to earn coins. Videos are shown only when you tap the button. They are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.

Because children may play too, every ad request uses Google's child treatment (COPPA child-directed treatment, and users under the age of consent under the GDPR), and only G-rated (all audiences) ads are allowed. In this mode Google does not show personalised or interest-based ads and does not use remarketing. The game does not use the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for tracking permission.

To deliver an ad, measure that it was shown and prevent fraud, Google receives limited technical information from your device, such as the IP address (used to estimate a general region), an app-specific device identifier (not the advertising identifier), device model and operating system, app version, whether the video was watched, and crash and performance data of the ad component. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no chat, no sharing and no way to enter personal information. Links that leave the game (this policy and support) are behind a short grown-up check. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, for example by writing to us, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you and delete them when they are no longer needed, at the latest after 12 months.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, KVKK in Türkiye, or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its use. Because we do not keep personal information about players, most requests can be met simply by deleting the game. For anything else, write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Security and international transfers

Information that Google receives for ads may be processed outside your country, under the safeguards described in Google’s policies. We do not run servers that store player data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
