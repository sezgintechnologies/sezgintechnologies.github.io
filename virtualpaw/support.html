Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your pet and progress are saved on your device only. There is no account and nothing is stored on our servers. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

How do I start over?

Open More → Settings → Start over. This deletes your pet and progress on this device.

How do I learn to play?

Open More → How to play for a short picture guide.

Why are there videos?

The game is free. You can choose to watch a short video to earn a few coins; videos never play by themselves. They are family-friendly (rated G) and not personalised.

Can my pet die?

No. If it is left alone for a long time it gets sad and hides in the garden shed. Come back, call it and look after it, and it will be happy again.

Notifications

The game can remind you when your pet needs you, at most a few times a day and never at night. You can switch reminders off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Languages

Virtual Paw speaks 27 languages and follows your phone's language. You can change it in Settings → Language.
